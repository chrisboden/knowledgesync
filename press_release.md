# Peregian Digital Hub Launches Groundbreaking AI Initiative to Transform Noosa's Digital Future

**Noosa, Queensland [Date]** - The Peregian Digital Hub, a Noosa Council initiative, today announced the launch of Tokenizer, a comprehensive AI upskilling and innovation program designed to position the Noosa region at the forefront of the artificial intelligence revolution.

Supported by the Queensland Government's Advance Queensland initiative, Regional Development Australia, Noosa Chamber of Commerce, and Queensland Computers, Tokenizer represents a significant investment in the region's digital future.

"AI is reshaping how we work and create," said [Spokesperson Name], [Title] at Peregian Digital Hub. "Tokenizer is our response to ensure our community not only adapts to this change but thrives in it. We're building on our track record of AI innovation, including our successful AI Lab which has already catalyzed the formation of ventures like Blackbird-funded Springboards.ai."

The program offers tailored pathways for diverse groups within the community:
- **Startups & Entrepreneurs**: Access to peer support, founder events, and mentoring
- **Business Leaders**: Workshops, 1:1 mentoring, and proof-of-concept development support
- **Digital Professionals**: Specialized upskilling in AI for marketers, creatives, and technologists
- **Youth & Community**: AI Cadetship program for teenagers and weekly Hub Inventors Club for children
- **Local Government**: Dedicated AI upskilling workshops for Council employees

Key program features include:
- Weekly AI workshops and talks in a state-of-the-art AI Lab
- Hands-on hackathons and AI residencies
- Expert mentoring from industry professionals
- Access to advanced AI development resources
- Community events and networking opportunities

"Tokenizer isn't just about learning AI; it's about actively shaping its future in our region," continued [Spokesperson Name]. "We're creating pathways for businesses to innovate, professionals to upskill, and our community to embrace the opportunities AI presents."

The program launches with strong support from local industry and a clear vision to make the Sunshine Coast a recognised centre of excellence for AI and robotics innovation.

Interested participants can learn more and register their interest at peregianhub.com/tokenizer.

### About Peregian Digital Hub
The Peregian Digital Hub is a Noosa Council initiative dedicated to building a thriving digital sector in the Noosa region. The Hub has become a hotbed of AI activity, hosting numerous workshops, talks, and events in its world-class AI Lab.
